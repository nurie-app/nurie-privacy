# Privacy Policy (nurie - Coloring for Kids)

Last updated: 2026-11-16

[日本語](../)

"nurie - Coloring for Kids" (shown as "Coloring" on the Home Screen; "the app") respects the privacy of children and their parents.
This policy explains the information the app handles.

## 1. Our approach: we collect nothing
The app **does not collect any information about you and does not send anything off your device**.

- The app has no servers of its own and does not connect to the internet.
- There are no ads, analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.
- There is no account or sign-in. The app never asks for a name, age, or email address.
- There are no in-app purchases.

## 2. Information stored on your device
- **Artworks**: the pictures you color or draw in "Free drawing", and the date they were drawn, are saved on your device so you can see them in the album and keep coloring later.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## 3. Device permissions
- **Photos (add only)**: used only when you choose "Save to Photos" on the "You did it!" screen and a parent answers the check, to save the artwork to the Photos app. The app never reads your photos.

You can turn permissions off at any time in the Settings app.

## 4. Parental check
Before anything that goes outside the app (such as saving to Photos) and before erasing artworks from the album, the app asks a parent to answer a math question.

## 5. Sharing with third parties
Because the app collects no information, there is nothing to share with third parties.

## 6. Changes
If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
