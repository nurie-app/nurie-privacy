FAQ

How do I play?

Choose a picture on the first screen, pick “Paint”, “Crayon” or “Rainbow” and a color, then trace with your finger. With “Stay inside” on, color only goes inside the area where you started. Use “Erase” to erase, and the Undo (←) button to go back.

Are my pictures saved?

Yes. They are saved automatically, so you can keep coloring next time. To color the same picture again, tap “New” after “Done!”. Your pictures are in the “Album”.

How do I erase pictures from the album?

In the album, tap “Tidy up” and answer the parent question. Then choose a picture and tap “Erase”. Erased pictures can't be restored.

How do I save to the Photos app?

Tap “Done!”, then “Save to Photos”. A math question for parents appears; enter the answer to save. The first time, please allow adding to Photos.

The app closes while my child is playing

Swiping from the screen edge can go to the Home Screen. Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access) keeps the app from switching.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. The app doesn't collect or send any information. See the privacy policy for details.
